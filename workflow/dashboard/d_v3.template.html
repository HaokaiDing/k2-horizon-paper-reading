    How these numbers are calculated

    • Total papers: the number of rows in the dataset.
    • From 2025 or later: rows whose publication year is 2025 or greater, divided by total rows, shown as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.
    • Robot-free collection: rows where robot_free_collection is yes, divided by total rows, as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.
    • Releases a dataset: rows where releases_dataset is yes, divided by total rows, as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.
    • Cross-embodiment: rows where cross_embodiment is yes, divided by total rows, as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.
    • Handheld-gripper papers: rows where collection_method is handheld_gripper. The sub-number is how many of those rows have robot_free_collection equal to yes. The percentage of handheld-gripper papers that are robot-free uses the handheld-gripper count as the denominator.
    • Median stated demos: the median of num_demonstrations across rows where that field is non-empty; if there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle values. The sub-number is how many rows state a value.
    • Method counts and shares: rows grouped by collection_method; share is the group count divided by total rows, as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.
    • Growth chart: rows grouped by publication year, then stacked by collection_method; each segment height is the number of papers that year using that method. The y-axis uses round ticks in steps of 20.
    • End-effector counts: rows grouped by end_effector.
    • Largest stated datasets: rows with a non-empty num_demonstrations, sorted from largest to smallest; the ten largest are listed.
